## About us / 关于我们

**MedX AI Lab** is a medical AI research lab affiliated with the National Engineering Research Center for Software Engineering at Peking University and supervised by Research Assistant Professor **Liantao Ma**. We work to build trustworthy, accessible AI for clinical decision-making and medical research, in collaboration with academic and clinical partners.

**医维矩阵人工智能实验室**依托北京大学软件工程国家工程研究中心，由**马连韬**研究型助理教授指导。我们聚焦人工智能与医学交叉研究，与高校及临床机构合作，致力于构建高可信、易用的医学 AI，赋能临床诊疗决策与医学科研。

## Core research directions / 主要研究方向

- **Medical foundation models and agents / 医疗大模型与智能体** — Multimodal medical foundation models, multi-agent collaboration for clinical decision-making, and research automation (Medical AI Scientist). / 多模态医疗大模型、多智能体协作临床决策与科研自动化（Medical AI Scientist）等。
- **Trustworthy medical AI / 可信医疗人工智能** — Interpretable deep learning for clinical prediction, human-AI collaboration, and interaction reliability. / 面向临床预测的可解释深度学习、人机协作与交互可靠性等。
- **AI for Science (medicine) / AI for Science（医学方向）** — EHR-driven scientific discovery, AI-assisted medication guidance, and evidence-based medicine. / 电子病历数据驱动的科学发现、AI 赋能用药指导、循证医学等。

## Featured projects / 特色项目

- **[HealthFlow](https://github.com/yhzhu99/HealthFlow)** — Multi-agent framework for automating electronic health record analysis.
- **[MedAgentBoard](https://github.com/yhzhu99/MedAgentBoard)** — Benchmark for medical multi-agent collaboration.
- **[ColaCare](https://github.com/PKU-AICare/ColaCare)** — LLM-driven multi-agent collaboration for EHR modeling.
- **[OneEHR](https://github.com/MedX-PKU/OneEHR)** — Toolkit for longitudinal EHR analysis and reproducible ML/DL/LLM experiments.
