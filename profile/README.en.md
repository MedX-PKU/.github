### About us

Affiliated with the **National Engineering Research Center for Software Engineering at Peking University** and supervised by **Research Assistant Professor Liantao Ma**, we work with academic and clinical partners on medical foundation models, trustworthy AI, and data-driven discovery. Our goal is to build reliable, accessible tools for clinical decision-making and medical research.

### Research directions

| Focus | What we work on |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Medical foundation models and agents** | Multimodal medical foundation models, multi-agent collaboration for clinical decisions, and research automation (Medical AI Scientist). |
| **Trustworthy medical AI** | Interpretable deep learning for clinical prediction, human-AI collaboration, and interaction reliability. |
| **AI for Science in medicine** | EHR-driven scientific discovery, AI-assisted medication guidance, and evidence-based medicine. |
