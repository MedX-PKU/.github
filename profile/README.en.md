## About us

MedX AI Lab is affiliated with the National Engineering Research Center for Software Engineering at Peking University and supervised by Research Assistant Professor Liantao Ma. We work at the intersection of AI and medicine, collaborating with academic and clinical partners to bring medical AI research into clinical practice.

## Core research directions

- **Medical foundation models and agents:** Multimodal medical foundation models, multi-agent collaboration for clinical decision-making, and research automation (Medical AI Scientist).
- **Trustworthy medical AI:** Interpretable deep learning for clinical prediction, human-AI collaboration, and interaction reliability.
- **AI for Science in medicine:** EHR-driven scientific discovery, AI-assisted medication guidance, and evidence-based medicine.
